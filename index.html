01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

A plot with a story.
A future to shape.

The Vantage began with a simple ambition: to build something we believed in, ourselves.

At 172A Shelford Road, the hillside setting inspired a home that steps with the land, opens towards the outlook and makes a distinctive arrival from the street.

We are now ready to pass that vision on. For a self-builder, developer or investor, the conversation starts with your ambitions, your appraisal and a deal that works for you.

Planning permission
granted.

Outline planning permission was granted by Gedling Borough Council on 28 March 2025 under planning reference 2024/0465.

The consent establishes the principle of residential development, with access, layout and scale approved. Appearance and landscaping are reserved for subsequent approval, subject to the conditions of the outline permission.
